# Legionus Maximus

A survivors-style game in pixel art where the player steers a growing Roman legion through barbarian hordes. Fourth game from Slow Sunday Games. The game is in `game/`.

## Layout

- Autoloads (`project.godot`): `Profile` (`profile.gd`, the saved profile), `Lang` (`lang.gd`, languages and fonts), `Audio` (`audio.gd`, sounds, and the music: the battle tracks `music/battle_*.ogg` played one after another from the moment the game opens, through the menus and runs alike, with any music in the player's own folder `user://music/`, which the settings open; the harp loop `calm.ogg` is only in the trailer), `Steamworks` (`steam.gd`, achievements and rich presence).
- `game/scripts/sim.gd`: the whole run as plain data (legion, horde, missiles, coins, levels). No nodes, so tests drive it directly. `legion.gd` is the soldiers and their formation, `horde.gd` the barbarians as packed arrays, elites, chieftains, chests and supply carts.
- `game/scripts/terrain.gd`: the props on the field as plain data, seeded per run: scatter, winding forest ridges (`_ridge()`, swept over one tree lattice shared by the whole field, numbers in `forest` in balance.json) and lines of rocks and logs. Trees, rocks and logs are solid; `refresh()` builds a grid of the solid ones around the commander and the horde reads it directly in `_move` for speed. `push_out()` with a time step records chopping on trees, `fell_ready()` turns trees chopped long enough into stumps (`felled`), and `posts` holds the palisade's, the wall's and the Gaulish king's stakes, in the order they rot. Wall stakes (`build_wall()`, kind `"wall"`, marked `Terrain.WALL` in `solid_tree`) stand behind the rear rank, and barbarians walking into one batter it (`batter()`, `hold` seconds each, bosses `boss_batter` times faster) instead of slipping through, so a wall across a narrow gap holds the horde back for a while; a crowd ringing a wall breaks in after about 20 to 25 seconds. A province with an entry in `fields` in balance.json (Gaul) has its own scatter and, in place of ridges, villages, east and west hedgerows and wheat fields (`_farmland()`); a village or hedge is drawn with no solid of its own and made solid by unseen blockers (kind `""`) over the ground it covers. `tools/art/build_atlas.py` brings the hedge pieces' greens to the oak's (`MATCH_GREEN`). Wheat fields can be walked through, by the legion and the horde alike, while their province's `solid` is off as it is in Gaul; with it on, each field's rectangle, set `field_inset` px in from its drawn edge with rounded corners (`field_solids()`), is listed in a third grid (`fd_*`) that `push_out`, the horde, Neptune's wave and the route map read, `out_of_fields()` keeps spawns out of the wheat, and a legion marching head-on into a field turns along its edge (`round_field()`). A field is cut back (`_fit_field()`) to keep a lane of `field_lane` px from the next field and from every hedge and house, so Gaul is never a maze. `clear()` empties the field for tests. It also keeps a route map (`update_flow()`, a breadth-first search over 32 px squares, redone when the commander moves a square) that a barbarian who bumps into a solid prop follows round it. Ravines (`ravine()`, one per 1800 px square, numbers in `ravines`) are gorge centre lines with half widths; `refresh()` lists their segments in a second grid (`rv_*`) that `push_out`, the horde and the route map read, and `bridges` holds the decks laid over them (`Sim.build_bridge`). The men file into rows for a deck (`Legion._column`) only while the commander is on it, heading for it within `bridge_aim` and `bridge_approach` px past the formation's front, or just stepping off one he crossed. The fort's gate uses the same rule from outside, so fighting along its wall keeps the formation. The HUD's edge badges (`Hud.targets_of`) point to the objectives, the fort and the living chieftain; `--boss=ID --boss-act=far` brings a chieftain in stunned off screen for a look at his badge. `--go-to=ravine` or `--go-to=bridge` with `--autoplay` starts at the lip of the nearest ravine, and `--go-to=ravine-end` just past one of its ends, for a look. `--go-to=field` stands the legion beside the wheat field nearest the start (with `--province=gaul`). A province with an entry in `mountains` (Dacia) swaps the scatter, the ridge tree (pines) and the line kinds (rock only) and raises the ridge and line chances; one with an entry in `streams` gets frozen streams laid out like ravines (`stream()`, kept clear of them), on which the legion slides (`Sim.glide`, `ice` in balance.json). `--go-to=stream` starts above the nearest one. A province with an entry in `deserts` (Parthia) has no ridges or lines: a region holds an oasis of palms, a ruined wall line (solid, sometimes breached), a spread of dunes or open sand (`_desert_feature()`), and dunes are soft sand that slows the march (`Terrain.on_dune`, `Sim.on_sand`). A province with an entry in `moors` (Britannia) has heather scatter and few ridges (`ridge_chance`); its other regions hold a stone circle, a spread of bog pools or a patch of heather (`_moor_feature()`). A circle is a ring of `stone_circle` pictures, each made solid by unseen blockers on its seven stones, with `circle_gap` of them left out as its opening; trees of ridges nearby are kept out of it (`_henges`). Bogs slow the march more than dunes (`Terrain.in_bog`, `Sim.in_bog`) and block nothing. `--go-to=circle` or `--go-to=bog` starts beside the nearest one.
- The pursuit (`pursuit` in balance.json, `Horde._pursue`): once a second the horde weighs the ground the commander marches for each kill against the stream; a legion that keeps moving while its arms do the killing, or never moves at all, raises `horde.pursuit` towards `cap`. From 1 up, beasts and sprinters (`_hunt`, carrying no coins and left out of the kill rate) come in ahead of its march and from all round it, tougher and fiercer as it climbs, the bosses come sooner and more of them may be alive. A legion that holds and fights (the facing bots) keeps it at 0.
- A province opens by winning the drawn one before it on the road (`Content.province_before()`, `Profile.open_won_provinces()`); the map shows it locked with what opens it until then, and a click on it shows that in the panel with the back sound. Each run has a difficulty, `run_setup.difficulty` (`Sim.DIFFICULTIES`: easy, medium, hard), saved in the profile settings like the province and chosen in the map's panel: Medium is the game as tuned, and Easy and Hard scale every enemy's health (`health_mult`), the stream of ordinary enemies (`spawn_mult`), the denarii (`denarii_mult`) and, where the block sets `damage_mult` (Easy and Medium do), every enemy's damage, by the `easy` and `hard` blocks in balance.json (`Horde.level_mult()`), on top of the province's own `difficulty`. A win on any difficulty counts as won and opens the next province; a win on Hard also sets `hard_won`, the red pennant on the map. The `crowd` block in balance.json caps the ordinary enemies on the screen or within `margin` of it at 70, 100 and 200 by difficulty (`Horde.crowd`, counted each step in `_spawn`): the stream slows from half that number and stops at it, and surges, war bands, hunters and summons are cut to fit (`Horde.full()`), while elites and the chieftain still come. Every enemy walks at `enemy_speed_mult` (0.9) of the speed content.json lists. An enemy on foot left beyond `give_up_radius` (600) and out of view gives up the chase and is removed (chariots and riders, `Horde.k_rides`, keep circling), with no kill or coin, so marching away is a real escape; `solid_range` is set past it so every enemy still chasing bumps into trunks and no crowd comes out of a tree line without felling it. At most `bosses.max_alive` (3) timed bosses are alive at once, and the pursuit neither hastens nor adds bosses (`pursuit.boss_haste` and `pursuit.bosses` are 0). Any soldier picks up a chest. The horn (`audio.kinds.surge`) has a 45 second cooldown, since hunts and the grove send bands every few seconds. Medium (20 November 2026) sends 80% of the enemies with 85% of their health and damage and pays 1.25 times the denarii; Easy and Hard set `xp_mult` so that per coin they level as fast as Medium. The first supply cart of a run shows a notice saying what carts give, and each opened cart names its reward. Endless and Decimatio open by winning in the province.
- Gaul opens by winning in Germania (`Content.province_before()` passes over provinces whose art is missing). Its enemies' abilities are keys in content.json read by `horde.gd`: the swordsman's `slash`, horseman's `flank_charge` (he rears on impact), archer's `volley` (a ring in `foe_lobs`), boar's `rush` (stunned by anything solid), champion's `duel`, horn blower's `rally` heal, `blast` and `flee`, and the king's `sworn` guard, `siege` stakes and `summon`, with his guards released in his second phase.
- Dacia opens by winning in Gaul. The falx warrior's `falx` blows ignore the testudo, armour and the shield of a covering man, the archers' `salvo` looses together on one drum (`salvo_cd`, arrows the testudo stops), the draco bearer's `howl` hastens the warriors round him, the bear's `maul` throws soldiers back, the falx band leader's `onrush` charges a marked lane with his band, the priest's `blizzard` leaves a storm (`storms`) that slows the march, and the king's `wall` of falx warriors turns blows from in front, with a telegraphed `sweep`, `rockfall` rings and, in his second phase, a `leap`.
- Parthia opens by winning in Dacia. The horse archer's `kite` rides off from soldiers who close in, shooting back, until his arrows are spent, the cataphract's `lance` is a long straight dash and his `armour` turns part of each arrow, the camel rider's `thrust` (a narrow slash) reaches the man behind, the slinger's `sling` stones leave soldiers `dazed`, the cataphract leader's `wedge` and the war elephant's `trample` are lane charges in `_onrush` (the wedge's riders spawn behind him; the trample shakes the screen) while the elephant's `tower` shoots, and the shah has a cataphract guard, an `arrow_storm` of horse archers whose volleys fall on marked rings, resupplied by a camel train that ends the storm if cut down, and calls in war elephants (`fury.call`) in his second phase. A kind with `scale` in content.json is drawn that much larger than its art; none uses it, since the chariot, scythed chariot and war elephant are drawn at full size (`chariot_72`, `chariot_charge_96`, `war_elephant_136`).
- Britannia, the last province, opens by winning in Parthia; the map's road sails to it from Parthia. The painted warrior's `fearless` charge is a dash that the crowd does not shove aside and ends in a battle cry that leaves the soldiers round him `afraid` (slower strikes, in `legion.gd`). The slinger's `salvo` of stones (`missile: stone`) looses together from long range and does not stun. The druid acolyte's `tend` heals the warriors round him. The chariot (`driver` behaviour, `Horde._pass`) passes beside the legion throwing javelins, swings round for another pass, and may set down two painted warriors (`drop`). The scythed chariot's `scythe` is an `_onrush` lane with `blade` reach that it drives `passes` times. The druid's `sky_fire` drops fire on a line of marked rings (`sky` lobs) and his `rouse` heals and hastens the warriors round him. The warrior queen's `drive` sweeps her along a marked curved path through the legion's outer ranks (`Horde.drive`), `tribes` brings war bands from several sides, and in her second phase `burn` marks rings that become burning ground (`storms` with `fire`).
- `game/scripts/objectives.gd`: the camp, stranded column and sacred grove placed in clearings at the start, and the fort that appears with the chieftain at `chieftain_at`, which can be held only once he has fallen (his death before the fort is in sight leaves it to hold); the triumph after a win lasts until every barbarian and coin is gone and every man is in his place, between `triumph.seconds` and `triumph.most_seconds`, and a win moves the map to the next province (`Game.next_province`); their huts, oaks and walls are terrain props (`Terrain.add_clearing`). `--objectives-near` places them close and `--go-to=camp|column|grove|fort` starts beside one (inside the fort; `--go-to=fort-gate` south of it, marching in through the gate), and `--go-to=burning-camp` in the camp's ring so it burns at once, for a look. The grove's ring of trees or stones is scenery with no solid part, since a legion standing at the altar is wider than the ring, and the first time the commander comes within `grove.tip_within` a notice says what burning it gives.

- `game/scripts/screens/`: title, camp, quartermaster, tablinum, map, commanders, pause, settings and the run summary, all drawn through `ui.gd`. `controls.gd` has the default keys and pad buttons and the player's rebinding laid over them.

## Art

All art is PixelLab, made with `tools/art/pixellab.py` (token in `.env`, never in code), costs in `tools/art/ledger.csv`.

- Characters are 48 px (the berserker, war band leader and war priest 80 px, the chieftain 112 px, all from their `_big` folders), low top-down, selective outline, from 8 directions. Walking and running use PixelLab's built-in `walking` and `running` motions, which loop and cost nothing.
- Ground and props are made with Pro Flash using `tools/art/raw/style_ref.png` (the commander and a barbarian) as the style image, or they come out in a brighter cartoon style that does not match.
- `tools/art/batch_art.sh` makes the prototype's art, `build_atlas.py` packs it into `game/assets/atlas.png` (8192 px wide, kept to one page so the horde draws in one batch, refused past 8192 tall) and takes new units and props through its `UNITS` and `PROPS`, a unit's art folder through `FOLDERS`, and an "attack" action wherever `attack_<direction>` folders hold frame_00 to frame_08; `make_seamless.py` and `remove_leaves.py` make the ground tile and `make_boot_splash.py` the boot splash.
- No mirroring: every direction is drawn.
- Look at every still before paying to animate it, and try one direction of a new animation before paying for all eight.
- Animals and riders use `--template dog`, `bear` or `horse`. The `horse` and `bear` templates can save a picture under another direction than the one it shows, often but not always one step clockwise, so check every still by eye, rename it to what it shows and keep PixelLab's own label in `character.json` as `pixellab_direction`.
- Icons use pixen with "inventory item icon of a single object: ... Object only, no people, no faces, no creatures", or it draws a figure. Ask for no symbols or crosses on props.
- When jobs run in parallel, log each job's own cost in `ledger.csv`, not the whole balance drop.

## Rules

- The horde is packed arrays with a flat grid; keep per-enemy work out of dictionaries, and check `--bench` after touching `_move_enemies`.
- Packed arrays copy when changed through another container, so change them by name.

## Release

- Pushing a tag like `v0.1.0` runs `.github/workflows/release.yml` ("Release builds"). The tag must match `config/version` in `game/project.godot` or the run stops. It can also be started by hand with `gh workflow run "Release builds"`.
- Jobs: the tests, then a full and a demo export for Windows, macOS and Linux, then macOS signing, then a GitHub release for tags. Demo builds come from the "... demo" presets, which carry the `demo` feature (`OS.has_feature("demo")`).
- The demo (the `demo` feature, or `--as-demo`) plays only `demo.province` in balance.json (Germania), lists the first `demo.commanders` (3) commanders and ends each run at `demo.seconds` (660, 11 minutes), through `game.gd`.
- Mac builds ship unsigned until the Apple secrets (`MACOS_CERTIFICATE_P12` and the rest, see `.github/scripts/sign_macos.sh`) are set on the repo.
- Steam uploads stay off until `tools/steam/steam.json` has real app and depot ids and the repo variable `STEAM_UPLOAD` is `true`. Every id is 0 for now.
- Local export: `python tools/export.py [--demo] [windows|macos|linux]`.
