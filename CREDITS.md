 The fonts are Silkscreen by Jason Kottke (`game/assets/fonts/OFL.txt`) and Fusion Pixel by TakWolf (`game/assets/fonts/fusion-pixel-OFL.txt`), both under the SIL Open Font Licence 1.1. Silkscreen draws English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese; Fusion Pixel's 10 px Latin font draws Polish, Turkish, Russian and Ukrainian, and its 8 px Chinese, Japanese and Korean fonts draw those languages. They include glyphs from Ark Pixel Font by TakWolf, Misaki by Num Kadoma, Galmuri by Lee Minseo, Boutique Bitmap by Luke Liu (fancy type foundry) and Miseki Bitmap by Mark Li, whose licences are in `game/assets/fonts/fusion-pixel-LICENSES/` (all SIL Open Font Licence 1.1, except Misaki, which is free to use, change and share, its terms in `misaki/misaki.txt`). The logo's lettering is drawn in Cinzel by the Cinzel Project Authors (`tools/art/Cinzel-OFL.txt`, SIL Open Font Licence 1.1); only the finished logo picture ships. The translations were made with Azure AI Translator. Art was made with PixelLab; what each image cost is in `tools/art/ledger.csv`.

The campaign map is drawn by `tools/art/make_map.py` from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) 1:50m data (land, rivers, lakes and named regions in `tools/art/geo/`). Natural Earth is in the public domain and asks for no credit; it is credited here anyway.
