For the Content Survey in Steamworks, which asks whether the game uses AI-generated content.

Answer: yes, pre-generated content (made during development). No content is generated while the game is played.

Text to paste into the description box:

Much of the game's pixel art was generated with PixelLab, an AI pixel-art tool, then cleaned up, edited and assembled for the game: the soldiers, barbarians and their animations, the ground, trees and props, the objectives, the bosses and the icons. The logo and the interface are drawn by the game's own code. The translations into 13 languages were made with machine translation. Much of the game's code and writing was made with the help of an AI coding assistant. The sound effects are recordings made by people, and the music is played by the game's own tools from harp and drum recordings made by people, all credited in the credits file that comes with the game. No AI generates content while you play.

Notes:
- What PixelLab made is listed picture by picture in tools/art/ledger.csv; the sounds and the music's recordings are
  listed in CREDITS.md.
- Read the survey's own wording when you fill it in. If it says code written with AI tools does not need declaring,
  the sentence about the code and writing can be left out.
- Steam shows this text on the store page, so keep it accurate if more art is generated or replaced later.
