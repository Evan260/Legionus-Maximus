[h2]You steer. The legion fights.[/h2]

Legionus Maximus is a survivors-style action game in pixel art. You lead a Roman legion across five provinces of the ancient world, from the forests of Germania to the moors of Britannia. You move the commander and nothing else. Every legionary stabs, every archer shoots and every weapon fires on its own, while the barbarians pour in from every side. Each level-up adds men, a weapon or a drill, so a run that starts with a handful of soldiers ends with a hundred men in formation holding off a screen full of enemies.

[h2]At a glance[/h2]
[list]
[*]One control that matters: where you march. The formation turns as you turn, shields to the front and archers behind.
[*]Five provinces, each with its own ground, its own warriors, its own elites and its own chieftain: Arminius in Germania, Vercingetorix in Gaul, Decebalus in Dacia, the shah of Parthia and Boudica in Britannia.
[*]Eight kinds of soldier to recruit, from legionaries and archers to cavalry, a medicus, a ballista crew and a centurion.
[*]From a handful of men to a legion of a hundred, every one of them on the field and fighting.
[*]Five gods of Rome to call on when their favour is full: lightning, an earthquake, war frenzy, a rain of fire and healing light.
[*]Fell trees for timber, drive palisades across the barbarians' path and lay bridges over the ravines.
[*]Retake the eagles, burn the sacred grove and march into the Roman fort to win the province.
[/list]

[h2]Five provinces on the road from Rome[/h2]
The campaign map follows the real geography of the empire. Win a province and the road opens to the next.
[list]
[*][b]Germania:[/b] dark forests in long ridges and deep ravines. Club warriors, spearmen, axe throwers and wolves, with the berserker, the war band leader and the war priest, and Arminius at their head.
[*][b]Gaul:[/b] farmland of wheat fields, hedges and villages. Swordsmen, horsemen, archers and charging boars, with the champion who calls your commander to a duel and the horn blower, under Vercingetorix and his sworn bodyguard.
[*][b]Dacia:[/b] snowy mountains and frozen streams the legion slides across. Falx warriors whose blades cut through shields, archers who loose together, bears from the high passes, the falx band leader who charges down a marked lane and the priest who calls a blizzard, under Decebalus behind his wall of falx warriors.
[*][b]Parthia:[/b] desert dunes that slow the march, oases and ruined walls. Horse archers who ride away shooting, armoured cataphracts, camel riders and slingers, the cataphract wedge and the war elephant, under the shah and his storm of arrows.
[*][b]Britannia:[/b] wet moorland, bogs and stone circles at the edge of the known world. Painted warriors, chariots, slingers and druids, the scythed chariot and the druid who calls fire from the sky, under Boudica in her war chariot.
[/list]

[h2]A legion that grows with every level[/h2]
Fallen barbarians drop denarii, and the denarii fill the level bar. Each level pauses the battle and offers three choices:
[list]
[*][b]Recruits:[/b] eight kinds of soldier, each with his own health. Legionaries hold the front, archers and slingers shoot from the rear, cavalry ride out at the flanks, a standard bearer quickens the men round him, a medicus heals the wounded, a ballista crew fires heavy bolts down a line and a centurion's shout stuns the enemies round him.
[*][b]Arms:[/b] twelve weapons that fire on their own, from pilum volleys, the scorpio and the onager to war dogs, fire pots, caltrops and a ring of torches. Each has five ranks.
[*][b]Forged forms:[/b] bring two arms to their last rank and they can be forged into one, such as Greek fire or the siege train. There are six to find.
[*][b]Drills:[/b] fifteen upgrades for the whole legion, from forced marches and better armour to reserves who replace the fallen.
[/list]
The front rank takes the blows meant for the men behind it, so a deep formation keeps its archers alive. Press the testudo when the axes and spears start to fly, and the shields lock for a few seconds.

[h2]Bosses that tell you what they are about to do[/h2]
Elites come on their own timer, more of them as the run goes on, and every province has its own. Every ability is shown on the ground before it lands, so a legion that is watching can step out of it.
[list]
[*][b]The berserker[/b] circles round to the archers at the back, charges, and spins in a whirlwind inside a red ring.
[*][b]The falx band leader[/b] marks a lane and charges down it with his warriors.
[*][b]The war elephant[/b] tramples down a marked line, throwing soldiers aside, while the archers in its tower shoot.
[*][b]The druid[/b] calls fire down on a line of marked rings across the legion.
[*][b]Each chieftain[/b] fights in his own way: Vercingetorix rings the legion with stakes, Decebalus brings rocks down from the heights and leaps onto the legion, the shah calls in his war elephants and Boudica drives her chariot through the outer ranks and sets the land on fire.
[/list]

[h2]The gods of Rome[/h2]
Every barbarian killed fills the favour of the gods. When it is full, call on them and choose one of three:
[list]
[*][b]Jupiter[/b] brings down a storm of lightning on everything on screen.
[*][b]Neptune[/b] shakes the earth and throws the horde back, some of them into the ravines.
[*][b]Mars[/b] fills the legion with war frenzy.
[*][b]Vulcan[/b] rains fire, and every barbarian it touches burns.
[*][b]Apollo[/b] heals the whole legion and brings back the fallen.
[/list]

[h2]Cut your own road[/h2]
In Germania the forest grows in long ridges too thick to pass, and deep ravines split the field into regions joined at narrow crossings, so there are only certain ways to go and a legion can be cornered. Soldiers pressed against a tree fell it, and every tree they bring down is a log of timber. Spend timber to drive a line of stakes across the barbarians' path, or to lay a plank bridge over a ravine and open a way no one else can take.

[h2]A mission, not just a timer[/h2]
Every run is a campaign to win back the eagle standards Rome has lost.
[list]
[*]Storm a barbarian camp and take back its eagle, then watch the huts burn.
[*]Reach a stranded column holding out round its supply cart, and its survivors join your ranks.
[*]Hold the sacred grove while war bands come to defend it, and burn it.
[*]At 12 minutes a Roman fort appears and the chieftain comes out to fight. March into the fort and hold it to win, while he does all he can to stop you.
[/list]

[h2]Between runs[/h2]
[list]
[*]The denarii you carry home buy permanent upgrades from the quartermaster.
[*]Six commanders to unlock, each starting the run his own way.
[*]Win a province to open its Hard, Endless and Decimatio modes.
[*]The tablinum keeps every soldier, weapon, enemy and god you have met, with a line of real history for each.
[*]A run summary with your time, kills, denarii, the damage each weapon did and the size of your legion over time.
[/list]
