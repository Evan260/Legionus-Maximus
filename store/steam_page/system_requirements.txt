These are estimates from what the game draws and how it runs on the development machine; they have not yet been
measured on old or low end computers. The busiest late runs, with a hundred soldiers and the full horde on screen,
are the part that needs the processor most.

WINDOWS

Minimum
OS: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
Processor: any quad-core processor from the last ten years
Memory: 2 GB RAM
Graphics: any graphics card or integrated graphics supporting OpenGL 3.3
Storage: 150 MB available space

Recommended
Processor: any quad-core processor from the last six years
Memory: 4 GB RAM
Additional notes: The game is 2D and about 50 MB.


MACOS

Minimum
OS: macOS 13 Ventura or later on Apple silicon, macOS 11 Big Sur or later on Intel
Processor: Apple silicon or a 64-bit Intel processor
Memory: 2 GB RAM
Graphics: any Mac that runs these versions of macOS
Storage: 250 MB available space

Recommended
Memory: 4 GB RAM
Additional notes: The game is 2D and runs natively on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.


LINUX / STEAMOS

Minimum
OS: a 64-bit distribution from the last five years, or SteamOS
Processor: any quad-core processor from the last ten years
Memory: 2 GB RAM
Graphics: any graphics card or integrated graphics supporting OpenGL 3.3
Storage: 150 MB available space

Recommended
Processor: any quad-core processor from the last six years
Memory: 4 GB RAM
Additional notes: The game is 2D.
